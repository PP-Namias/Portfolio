# Security Policy for namias.tech

## Reporting a Vulnerability

If you discover a security vulnerability in the PP Namias portfolio website
(https://namias.tech), please report it privately.

**Contact:** pp.namias@gmail.com
**PGP Fingerprint:** (Contact for current PGP key)

## Scope

In-scope targets:
- https://namias.tech (production)
- https://*.namias.tech (subdomains)
- https://pp-namias.vercel.app (preview deployments)

Out of scope:
- Third-party services (Sanity CMS, Cal.com, Umami, Google)
- Denial of Service attacks
- Social engineering
- Physical attacks

## Disclosure Policy

1. Report vulnerabilities privately to the contact above.
2. Allow reasonable time for remediation (typically 90 days for critical, 180 for standard).
3. Do not disclose the vulnerability publicly until it has been remediated.
4. We will acknowledge receipt within 48 hours.

## Recognition

We maintain a hall of fame for security researchers who report valid vulnerabilities.
Please include your preferred attribution name when reporting.

## Safe Harbor

We will not take legal action against researchers who:
- Follow this disclosure policy
- Make a good faith effort to avoid privacy violations
- Do not cause damage to the site or its data
- Do not access or modify user data without consent

## Supported Versions

| Version | Supported |
|---|---|
| main (production) | ✅ |
| Preview deployments | ⚠️ (limited support) |

## Automated Scanning

This site is regularly scanned using PentestAgent (https://github.com/GH05TCREW/pentestagent)
for automated security testing. Scans are conducted from CI/CD pipelines and respect
rate limits and robots.txt.
