# Use Content Health Inspector

## What it does
The **Content Health** inspector lives in the right panel of every
document. It shows:
- Document age in days
- Number of broken references (deep)
- Whether the document is featured
- Whether the document is overdue for review

## How to read it
1. **Open any document**.
2. **Click the right rail → Content Health**.
3. The inspector shows a green/yellow/red card. Red = action required.

## How to fix
Follow the recommendations in the card. Most often: update `updatedAt`
by making any edit and re-publishing.
