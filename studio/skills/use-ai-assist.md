# Use AI Assist

## What it does
Sanity's AI Assist is a field-level AI helper. You can use it to:
- Generate or rewrite copy
- Translate
- Generate alt text
- Fix grammar
- Make text shorter/longer

## Steps

1. **Click on a text field** in any document (title, summary, body, etc.).
2. **Click the sparkle icon** that appears in the field toolbar.
3. **Pick an instruction** from the dropdown (e.g. "Make this more concise").
4. **Review** the suggestion. Accept or reject.
5. **Custom instructions** — type your own in the input field.

## AI Credit tracking
AI Assist uses your project's AI credits. The **Dashboard** tool shows
remaining credits.

## Common mistakes
- ❌ Using AI to generate facts you don't verify (always proofread).
- ❌ Using AI to write entire blog posts without editing.
