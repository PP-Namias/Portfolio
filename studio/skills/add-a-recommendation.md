# Add a Recommendation

## Steps

1. **Content → Pages → Profile → Recommendations** → **Create new**.
2. Fields:
   - **Quote** — required, 5 rows of text.
   - **Name** — required.
   - **Title** — their job title.
   - **Relationship** — how they worked with you.
   - **Company** — company name.
   - **Company URL** — optional, https.
   - **Featured** — toggle to show in the homepage recommendations.
   - **Avatar** — optional image with hotspot.
3. **Publish** — appears on `/#recommendations` in the profile section.

## Common mistakes
- ❌ Quoting more than 4 lines — the card layout breaks.
